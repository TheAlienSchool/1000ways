About the Sanctuary

The Gamelatron is a globally recognized sound art installation featuring autonomous bronze sculptures that create unique sonic landscapes. 1000 Ways to Sit transforms this technology into a contemplative sanctuary for San Francisco's conscious community.

Located at Heron Arts, this 10-week festival of stillness offers multiple pathways into deep listening, meditation, and community connection. Each visit creates soundscapes that have never existed before and will never exist again.

The Forms You Are Sitting With

Gamelatron Via Oro

The Way of Gold

44 bronze and steel instruments mounted in a U-shaped embrace around you. This is the voice of the sanctuary. The algorithm that composes these sounds ranges from barely audible whispers to communal resonance, responding to your presence in the space.

Body Phones 38Hz

Headphones for Your Whole Body

Two 5-foot gongs tuned to the frequency of deep cellular calm. When that gong strikes at 38 Hz, something changes in your body at the cellular level. This frequency resonates with patterns in your nervous system—the same states that occur when you feel deeply calm or speak your truth.

Gamelatron Amoghasi

The Unfailing Accomplisher

8 hand-forged bronze gongs, each a unique voice in the sonic conversation. Amoghasi means "the unfailing accomplisher"—and indeed, these gongs accomplish what words cannot: direct transmission of vibrational truth to your nervous system.

"Your presence is an essential part of how this experience functions. The acoustic space you're sitting in is being co-created by your presence, and their presence, and the presence of this intentional sonic sanctuary."

Your Presence IS the Support

"The most vital support you can offer this sanctuary is your presence."

Every person who sits intentionally, who breathes with awareness, who notices the connection between gong strike and heartbeat is proving something important: that spaces can be designed to support human flourishing rather than extract from it.

The Gamelatron responds to your presence. Your vibration becomes part of the composition. By experiencing the sanctuary, you become living proof of its value—and that experiential support is what sustains this offering for San Francisco's conscious community.
